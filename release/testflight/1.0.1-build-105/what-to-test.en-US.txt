This build improves audio-route recovery and FM-DX scanner result saving.

The most important check is whether receiver audio returns to the phone speaker instead of disappearing.

Please test the FM-DX band scanner on an FM-DX server.

Please also test quick new-signal scanning.
The most important check is whether the app still shows new signals compared with the saved baseline while keeping the complete saved-results database.
