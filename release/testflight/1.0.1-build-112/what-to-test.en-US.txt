This build adds receiver-specific favorite settings and faster KiwiSDR passband control.

- add a receiver to favorites, change its frequency, mode, tuning step, and signal-processing options, disconnect, and reconnect
- verify that the favorite receiver settings are restored and are not overwritten by the initial server synchronization
- test the accessible 100 Hz narrow and widen passband controls for KiwiSDR, including VoiceOver navigation
- verify that an explicit history restore takes precedence over settings saved with the favorite receiver
- test FM-DX: the saved audio mode, antenna, and bandwidth should be applied after connecting
- verify that older favorite receivers without saved settings continue to load correctly

No user data or private keys are included in the application.
