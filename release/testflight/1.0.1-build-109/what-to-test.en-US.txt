This build adds FM-DX server administration and accessibility improvements.

Please test the following:

- Open a saved FM-DX profile, open its actions, and choose "Administer server". Verify that a valid administrator password opens the FM-DX setup panel and that the panel remains usable with VoiceOver.
- Verify that a missing password, an invalid password, or a tune-only password produces a clear error without opening the administration panel.
- Verify that KiwiSDR and OpenWebRX profiles do not expose FM-DX administration actions.
- On the receiver screen, navigate to the exact-frequency field with VoiceOver and verify that it is announced as an input for entering an exact frequency and remains easy to activate.
- Check Polish, English, German, Czech, Slovak, Spanish, French, Italian, Russian, and Simplified Chinese localization around FM-DX administration and the frequency field.
- As a regression check, start the app, connect to and disconnect from FM-DX, KiwiSDR, and OpenWebRX receivers, and verify that audio and basic controls continue to work.

Please report the device model, iOS version, receiver type and server version with any issue. Do not include passwords or session cookies in reports.
